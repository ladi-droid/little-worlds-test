How it works

Their own game, created by you

You pick a few things and we make a game with them as the hero. You get your own link to open on your phone or the family tablet.

1

Create the look

Pick their hairstyle, outfit, trainers and accessories. You’ll see them change on screen as you go.

2

Pick their trips

Choose up to five places to run through, from busy market streets to desert dunes. Swap them whenever you like.

3

Play on your phone or the family tablet

Add your link to your home screen and it gets its own icon. Nothing to download.

4

Try it before you buy

Run a short practice street first, learning to run, jump and lean. If they love it, join to open up their trips.

So many combinations

No two heroes look the same

You can mix hairstyles, outfits, trainers and accessories into thousands of looks. Make them look like themselves, or like their superhero self.

The map keeps growing

A whole world of trips

Pick a city you’ve visited together, or one you want to show them one day.
